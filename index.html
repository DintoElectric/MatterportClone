      Panoramas

      Add 360° photos, then place each one on the floor where it was taken. Tap a blue pin in the model, or a ring inside a photo, to move between them.

        Use JPEG files exported from Insta360 Studio. Raw INSP files are usually unstitched and can show up as two circles. Placements are remembered in this browser for this scan.

        Align panorama

        Turn the heading until the mesh lines up with the walls in the photo, then adjust the lens height until the floor and ceiling lines match.
